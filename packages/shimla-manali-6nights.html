Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Delhi/Chandigarh to Shimla

Start your Himalayan adventure! Depart from Delhi or Chandigarh early morning and drive to the Queen of Hills - Shimla. Upon arrival, check into your hotel. In the evening, take a leisurely walk on the famous Mall Road, enjoying street shopping and local cafes.

Distance: 240 km | Travel Time: 5-6 hours

Day 2: Shimla - Kufri - Local Sightseeing

After breakfast, explore Shimla's iconic attractions including Ridge Ground, Christ Church, and Jakhoo Temple. Visit Kufri for horse riding and panoramic Himalayan views. Return to Shimla for a relaxing evening at Mall Road.

Highlights: Ridge, Christ Church, Jakhoo Temple, Kufri Orchard, Green Valley

Day 3: Shimla to Manali

Depart Shimla for Manali, driving through scenic mountain landscapes. Stop at Narkanda for refreshment. Arrive in Manali and check into your hotel. Spend the evening exploring local markets and getting accustomed to the mountain town's charm.

Distance: 210 km | Travel Time: 5-6 hours

Day 4: Manali Local Sightseeing

Explore Manali's main attractions including Hadimba Temple, the picturesque Van Vihar, and the spiritual Tibetan Monastery. Visit the vibrant Mall Road for shopping and dining. Experience the beautiful Club House and relax by the Beas River.

Highlights: Hadimba Temple, Tibetan Monastery, Van Vihar, Mall Road, Club House

Day 5: Solang Valley & Atal Tunnel Adventure

Visit the thrilling Solang Valley for adventure activities including paragliding, zorbing, and skiing (seasonal). Marvel at the engineering feat - the Atal Tunnel Rohtang. Visit the stunning Sissu Lake. This day combines adventure with natural beauty.

Highlights: Solang Valley, Atal Tunnel, Sissu Lake, Adventure Activities

Day 6: Manali to Chandigarh/Delhi Drop

After breakfast, begin your return journey to Chandigarh or Delhi. Stop at scenic viewpoints and local markets along the way. Arrive by evening and drop off at your destination.

Distance: 280 km | Travel Time: 6-7 hours
