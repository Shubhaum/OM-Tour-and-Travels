Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Leh - Acclimatization

Arrive at Leh Ladakh airport (one of the highest in India at 10,682 ft). Get transferred to your hotel. Spend the day acclimatizing to the high altitude. Explore the local market and the old town. Early dinner and rest recommended.

Altitude: 11,562 ft | Activity: Light exploring only

Day 2: Leh Local Sightseeing

After acclimatization, start exploring Leh. Visit the magnificent Leh Palace, the peaceful Shanti Stupa with panoramic views, and important monasteries. Explore the vibrant local markets filled with traditional crafts and souvenirs.

Highlights: Leh Palace, Shanti Stupa, Local Monasteries, Old Town Market

Day 3: Khardung La Pass & Nubra Valley

Experience one of the world's highest motorable passes - Khardung La at 18,380 ft. Enjoy breathtaking Himalayan views. Visit Nubra Valley, known for its unique two-humped camels. Explore Diskit Monastery with its 32-meter Maitreya Buddha statue. Stay overnight in Nubra Valley or return to Leh.

Distance: 120 km | Highlights: Khardung La, Nubra Valley, Diskit Monastery, Camel Safari

Day 4: Pangong Lake Expedition

Embark on an unforgettable journey to Pangong Lake, one of the highest altitude lakes in the world. The lake is famous for its ever-changing colors - from deep blue to emerald green. Visit Magnetic Hill where vehicles appear to move uphill on flat ground. Experience the serene beauty of the lake as the sun sets.

Distance: 160 km | Altitude: 14,270 ft | Highlights: Magnetic Hill, Pangong Lake, Tso Moriri Lake (optional)

Day 5: Pangong Lake Sunrise & Sangam Point

Wake up early to witness the magical sunrise at Pangong Lake. Explore more of the lake and its surrounding areas. Visit Sangam Point where the Indus River meets another water body. Return to Leh with stops at scenic viewpoints.

Highlights: Pangong Lake Sunrise, Sangam Point, Photography opportunities

Day 6: Relaxation & Optional Activities

Today is your chance to relax and explore at your own pace. Optional activities include visiting local villages, shopping in Leh bazaar, visiting monasteries, or enjoying adventure activities like trekking or rafting. Enjoy traditional Ladakhi cuisine.

Suggestions: Hemis Monastery, Thiksey Monastery, Rafting, Local Market

Day 7: Departure

Enjoy a leisurely breakfast before heading to Leh airport for your flight back home. Carry memories of the incredible landscapes and experiences. Safe travels!

Note: Flight timings will determine your departure schedule

Important Information

🏥 Altitude Acclimatization

Leh is at high altitude (11,562 ft). Take it easy on Day 1, drink plenty of water, and consult your doctor if you have altitude-related concerns.

🧥 Weather & Clothing

Temperature can drop below freezing, especially at higher altitudes. Pack warm clothes, jackets, gloves, hats, and sun protection.

📱 Connectivity

Mobile connectivity is available in most areas. Stay connected with OM Tour & Travels for 24/7 support.

✈️ Flight Bookings

Book flights in advance. We provide airport transfer services. Let us know your flight details for smooth coordination.

🗺️ Best Time to Visit

Best season: June to September. Roads are clear and weather is favorable. Winter can be harsh with snow-blocked passes.

🎫 Documents Required

Valid ID, travel permits for restricted areas. OM Tour & Travels assists with permit processes.
